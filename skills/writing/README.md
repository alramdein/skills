# Writing

Skills for writing prose for people to read.

- **[blog-writer](./blog-writer/SKILL.md)**: Writes personal blog posts from your own experience, in plain everyday words, told as a story with honest results. Asks for the facts first and never invents them. Defaults to technical topics but works for any. Add your own format rules in [`references/format-rules/`](./blog-writer/references/format-rules/) and example posts in [`references/examples/`](./blog-writer/references/examples/).
